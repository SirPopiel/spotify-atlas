What's behind the numbers

How the numbers work

Minutes add up actual ms_played, including short listens. Plays only count listens above your selected threshold (30 seconds by default). Dates use each listening event’s end timestamp in your chosen time zone.

Session boundaries use 30-minute idle gaps. The first and last observed year/month may be partial. Missing periods mean no recorded activity in these files, not necessarily that you listened to nothing. Metadata does not include reliable genre information.

Only known Spotify streaming fields are kept in memory. The page never collects or uploads IP addresses, usernames, email addresses, or Spotify credentials. Reload the page to clear your data.
